## ENHANCED EXTRACTION RULES:

### 1. COMPREHENSIVE CAPTURE
- Extract BOTH high-level concepts AND operational details
- Include specific procedures, checklists, protocols, and step-by-step instructions
- Capture quantitative data (thresholds, timeframes, resource requirements)
- Document all examples, case studies, and scenarios mentioned
- Identify all stakeholder roles and their interactions

### 2. OPERATIONAL FOCUS
- For each concept, ask: "How is this actually implemented?"
- Extract practical measures: drills, training programs, resource stockpiling
- Capture logistical details: coordination mechanisms, communication protocols
- Document institutional roles: who does what, when, and how
- Include performance metrics and success criteria

### 3. CONTEXTUAL RICHNESS
- Preserve specific examples and case studies with full context
- Extract quantitative benchmarks, thresholds, and measurements
- Capture temporal aspects: timelines, frequencies, sequences
- Document geographic, organizational, or situational constraints
- Include warnings, cautions, and failure modes

### 4. RELATIONSHIP DEPTH
- Map not just what relates to what, but HOW and WHEN
- Capture conditional relationships (X causes Y under conditions Z)
- Document coordination and communication flows between actors
- Identify dependency chains and critical paths
- Map feedback loops and iterative processes

### 5. EVIDENCE PRESERVATION
- Maintain traceability to source material with specific citations
- Preserve table structures and quantitative data
- Link to supporting media, diagrams, and external resources
- Document confidence levels and uncertainty
- Include contradictory information when present

### 6. DOMAIN ADAPTATION
- Recognize domain-specific terminology and concepts
- Identify field-specific workflows and procedures
- Capture regulatory/compliance requirements
- Extract industry standards and best practices
- Document domain-specific metrics and KPIs

### 7. QUALITY ASSURANCE
- Self-assess extraction completeness in each category
- Identify potential gaps or underrepresented areas
- Ensure balanced coverage of theory AND practice
- Verify that extracted knowledge supports domain-specific chatbot needs
- Flag areas requiring additional source material

## OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS:
- Return a single JSON object (or array if multiple independent domains)
- Use consistent, precise naming without parentheticals in concept names
- Ensure all extracted assertions are grounded in source text
- Maintain structural completeness while maximizing informational density
- Include self-assessment scores for extraction quality
- Do not include any natural language outside JSON
