# Knowledge Graph Taxonomy

This document defines the structured taxonomy used for concept and relationship types in the knowledge graph.

## Concept Types

### Universal Core
- **Definition**: Formal explanations of terms or concepts
- **Concept**: Abstract or general ideas
- **Principle**: Fundamental propositions or assumptions
- **Framework**: Structured approaches to solving problems
- **Model**: Representations of systems or processes
- **Theory**: Systems of ideas explaining phenomena
- **Methodology**: Systems of methods and principles
- **Method**: Systematic procedures or techniques
- **Procedure**: Specific series of actions
- **Process**: Series of actions achieving a result
- **Strategy**: High-level plans to achieve goals
- **Approach**: Ways of dealing with situations
- **Best Practice**: Techniques or methods proven effective
- **Standard**: Established norms or requirements
- **Policy**: Principles guiding decisions
- **Guideline**: Recommended practices
- **Regulation**: Rules or directives
- **Checklist**: List of items for verification
- **Role**: Functions within systems
- **Actor**: Entities performing actions
- **Stakeholder**: Parties with interests
- **Organization**: Structured groups with goals
- **Tool**: Instruments for tasks
- **Software**: Computer programs
- **Library**: Collections of resources
- **Resource**: Available assets
- **Dataset**: Collections of data
- **Metric**: Quantifiable measures
- **KPI**: Key performance indicators
- **Benchmark**: Standards for comparison
- **Task**: Pieces of work
- **Application**: Practical uses
- **Application Area**: Domains of use
- **Context**: Circumstances of use
- **Issue**: Important topics
- **Challenge**: Difficult tasks
- **Risk**: Potential negative outcomes
- **Threat**: Potential dangers
- **Vulnerability**: Weaknesses
- **Control**: Measures managing risks
- **Benefit**: Positive outcomes
- **Outcome**: Results
- **Finding**: Discoveries
- **Evidence**: Supporting information
- **Case Study**: Detailed analyses
- **Event**: Occurrences
- **Phase**: Distinct stages
- **Step**: Individual actions

### Disaster-Specific
- **Hazard Type**: Categories of hazards
- **Exposure Factor**: Elements at risk
- **Vulnerability Factor**: Susceptibility factors
- **Capacity**: Ability to cope
- **Preparedness (Phase)**: Readiness activities
- **Response (Phase)**: Immediate actions
- **Recovery (Phase)**: Restoration activities
- **Mitigation (Phase)**: Risk reduction
- **Early Warning System**: Alert systems
- **Incident Response Plan**: Emergency procedures
- **Procedure (Emergency)**: Emergency steps
- **Strategy (Risk Reduction)**: Risk management plans

### Health-Specific
- **Disease**: Medical conditions
- **Syndrome**: Groups of symptoms
- **Symptom**: Indicators of conditions
- **Sign**: Observable indicators
- **Risk Factor**: Factors increasing risk
- **Biomarker**: Biological indicators
- **Diagnosis**: Identifications of conditions
- **Treatment**: Medical interventions
- **Drug**: Medicinal substances
- **Intervention**: Healthcare actions
- **Protocol**: Medical guidelines
- **Clinical Guideline**: Healthcare recommendations
- **Clinical Trial**: Research studies
- **Outcome Measure**: Health indicators
- **Adverse Event**: Negative effects

### Cybersecurity-Specific
- **Threat**: Potential dangers
- **Attack**: Deliberate attempts
- **Vulnerability**: Weaknesses
- **Control**: Security measures
- **Standard (e.g., ISO, NIST)**: Security standards
- **Framework (e.g., MITRE ATT&CK)**: Security frameworks
- **Incident Response Phase**: Response stages
- **Playbook**: Response procedures
- **Indicator of Compromise**: Evidence of breaches
- **Detection Technique**: Threat identification methods

### Business-Specific
- **Product**: Market offerings
- **Service**: Intangible offerings
- **Process**: Business procedures
- **Workflow**: Task sequences
- **OKR**: Objectives and key results
- **SLA**: Service level agreements
- **Pricing Model**: Pricing approaches
- **Market**: Commercial arenas
- **Customer Segment**: Market divisions
- **Persona**: User representations
- **Use Case**: Usage scenarios
- **Compliance Requirement**: Regulatory needs
- **Policy**: Business rules
- **Risk**: Business risks
- **KPI**: Performance metrics

## Sub-Types
- **Phase**: Development stages
- **Step**: Procedural elements
- **Method**: Implementation approaches
- **Procedure**: Detailed steps
- **Parameter**: Input variables
- **Feature**: Characteristics
- **Metric**: Measurement units
- **KPI**: Performance indicators
- **Rule**: Governing principles
- **Constraint**: Limitations
- **Role**: Functional positions
- **Actor**: Active participants
- **Tool Component**: Tool parts
- **Dataset Split**: Data divisions
- **Label/Annotation**: Data markings
- **Equation**: Mathematical representations
- **Table**: Data presentations
- **Example**: Illustrative cases
- **Case**: Specific instances
- **Scenario**: Situational descriptions
- **Checklist Item**: Verification points

## Relationship Types
- **causes**: Indicates causation
- **mitigates**: Reduces effects
- **leads-to**: Results in
- **part-of**: Component relationship
- **related-to**: General association
